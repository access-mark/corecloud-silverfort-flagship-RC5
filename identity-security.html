Where protection breaks down

Identity risk extends far beyond the cloud sign-in screen.

Identity protection needs to follow the authentication path, including infrastructure that cannot host modern agents or interactive prompts.

01 · Legacy authentication

Active Directory and older protocols

Many enterprises still depend on Kerberos, NTLM, LDAP, Windows infrastructure, file shares and applications built before modern MFA became standard. Evaluate which access paths remain exposed and where policy enforcement is technically supported.

02 · Machine identity

Service accounts and automation

Background services, integrations, scheduled tasks and application workloads authenticate without a person present. Inventory these identities, identify owners, establish expected activity and restrict unexpected authentication behaviour.

03 · Elevated access

Privileged and third-party access

Administrative identities can provide broad reach across an estate. Assess domain administrators, remote administration and supplier access with a focus on preventing misuse and reducing lateral movement.
